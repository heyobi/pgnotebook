# Tuple — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Tuple does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **No account, no sign-in, no network.** The app makes no network requests. PostgreSQL runs on your device.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Databases and notebooks are stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone
  (notebooks are visible to you in the Files app). They are deleted when you delete the app.
- **Sharing is your choice.** Data only leaves the device when you explicitly share or export a file
  (for example a notebook, a CSV or a database backup) through the iOS share sheet.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.**
- **TestFlight:** if you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to
  send with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

Contact: open an issue at <https://github.com/heyobi/pgnotebook/issues>.
